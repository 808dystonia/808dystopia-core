About

808 Dystopia

Underground hip-hop news and history. Accurate coverage. No fluff.

808 Dystopia is a media project covering current hip-hop news, catalog history, producers, and emerging artists. This domain is the public home: news board, product launches, and legal pages for the brand.

What we publish

  • News on underground and wider hip-hop
  • History posts and Reels on records, scenes, and producers
  • Album-cover and visual research on Pinterest
