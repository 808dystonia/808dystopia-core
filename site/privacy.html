Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Last updated September 6, 2026

This policy explains what 808 Dystopia collects on this site and when we connect official brand accounts to posting tools.

1. Short version

This site does not run accounts, checkouts, or a membership database. We do not sell personal information. If you only read the public pages, we get the usual hosting logs (IP, browser, page requested) from our host.

2. Site logs

The site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may log technical data needed to serve pages: IP address, user agent, requested URL, and time. We use that only to keep the site up and to debug problems. We do not use it to build marketing profiles.

3. Cookies

The public pages do not set advertising cookies. A host or CDN cookie may appear for security or load balancing. Social links take you to Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest, or Discord, which have their own cookies and policies.

4. Official social accounts

808 Dystopia operates public brand accounts. If you comment, DM, follow, or otherwise interact there, that platform — not this website — holds that activity under its own privacy policy.

5. Connected posting apps

We may connect official 808 Dystopia accounts to tools such as Composio so the team can publish content we created. Those connections use OAuth tokens for the brand accounts only. Tokens are used to post, schedule, or read basic account metadata. We do not use those APIs to collect your personal TikTok, X, or Instagram data from this website.

6. TikTok and X developer apps

If a TikTok or X developer app is registered to 808 Dystopia, its purpose is brand publishing and login for official operators. Redirect URLs and verification files on this domain exist only to prove we own 808dystopia.win. They are not a consumer login product.

7. Retention

Hosting logs follow the host’s default retention. OAuth tokens for brand accounts stay until we disconnect the app or they expire.

8. Children

This site is general media information. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children through these pages.

9. Requests

To ask what data we hold from this site, message an official channel listed on the home page. Platform-specific data (TikTok comments, X DMs, and so on) has to be requested from that platform.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top is the current version.
